What this prototype does

It is a read-only snapshot of sls issue data (stores issues, statuses Open/Approved/Closed/Pending, valued at the receipt-lot rate the stock, ledger and consumption reports use). Every option on the page recomputes on your phone; nothing is written anywhere. The real report will run the same rules live in the Inventory reports hub.

Classification

  • Value rank: items sorted by total issue value in the selected years and expense types. Fixed buckets: rank 1–N = A, N+1–2N = B, … and everything after the named classes falls in the last class. Pareto: A until the cumulative value share reaches the first threshold, B until the second, C after that.
  • Qty rank: the same rule applied to total issued quantity, either within each unit of measure (default, because 5 lakh stickers in PCS and 5 lakh litres of oil are not comparable) or across all items.
  • Exclude names/groups containing (default "CHARGES, MISC EXPNS") removes service and expense lines such as "Other Charges" or repair charges that are booked through stores but are not stock to buy. Clear it to rank everything.
  • Years active = calendar years with an issue; months active = distinct months with an issue in the selected years.
  • Trend compares the average of the last two selected years with the average of the earlier selected years (missing years count as zero). Up / Down beyond ±15%, otherwise Flat. "New" = issued only in the recent years.

Suggested purchase

  • Avg annual qty = total qty ÷ years active (not ÷ 5, so an item used in 3 of 5 years is not diluted).
  • Projected need = avg annual qty × horizon ÷ 12. Safety = avg annual qty × safety months ÷ 12.
  • Suggested qty = projected + safety − current stock − pending PO − pending indent (the last two only when "deduct pending" is on), floored at 0. Stock, pending PO and pending indent are today's position at the branch, computed from base tables (not the min-max views).
  • Only items issued since (default 2022) drops items whose last issue at the branch is older: while the mill ran normally in 2021–2023 an item not issued after 2021 was most likely replaced by another code (the duplicate oil masters are the clearest case). Set it to "any time" to see everything.
  • Suggested value = suggested qty × latest approved receipt rate for the item in the company (falls back to the historic average rate when the item was never received).

Known data caveats seen in this snapshot

  • 2024 is already a weak year (about a third of 2021–2023), and 2020 is low as well. Toggle years off to test a 2021–2023 baseline.
  • Capital and Overhauling are off by default: one-off capital issues (PV module structure, loom spares) otherwise land in class A.
  • Duplicate item masters split consumption (e.g. "SERVO ECOFIN J" vs "SERVO ECO FIN J"). Search "SERVO" to see them side by side.
  • Branch 4 belongs to company 1 and has no issues after 2024; branch 29 is company 2 and is the live factory.

Options to decide for the real build

  1. Class rule and bucket size (and how many named classes).
  2. Quantity classes within UOM, overall, or not at all.
  3. Expense types excluded by default.
  4. Default baseline years.
  5. Planning horizon and safety months defaults.
  6. Whether to deduct pending PO / indent by default.
  7. Store the class on the item (for indent/PO screens) or compute on the fly.
